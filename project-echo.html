Project context and objectives

09/2026 – present · 2 mos · TU Delft · Ongoing investigation

I initiated the skin team and am Skin Team Lead for RSA Project Echo. The wider project is preparing for RoboCup Rescue in April 2029. The skin work is an ongoing investigation; no Echo skin prototype performance or experimental results are claimed here.

Starting and leading the skin team

Project Echo is a student-led humanoid robotics project at TU Delft, part of the Robotics Student Association (RSA). It is preparing for RoboCup Rescue, a competition testing robots in simulated disaster-response scenarios.

I started the skin team and am recruiting new members while evaluating how robotic skin could enhance the humanoid’s performance in this challenge.

Sensing and material investigation

The technical objectives are scalable whole-body touch localisation, approximate force sensing and a compliant protective skin. The work investigates suitable sensing and material concepts, considering how they could be integrated across a humanoid body.

These objectives guide the ongoing investigation. Sensing concepts, materials and integration choices remain under evaluation.

Current status and next steps

The current focus is team recruitment and concept evaluation. Design decisions, prototypes and experimental results will be documented here as the work progresses.

My earlier Textile-Based Tactile Sensor (BSc) was a completed feasibility study with Fabio Jain at TH Köln. Its experimental results describe that prototype and do not establish performance for Echo.
